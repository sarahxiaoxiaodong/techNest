03 · CONNECT

Support should be easier to reach.

HealthBridge focuses on two groups who can benefit from stronger connections.

♡

FOR VOLUNTEERS

Share your time and knowledge.

Volunteers, healthcare students, and retired professionals can help seniors feel more confident navigating health information.

  • Help explain general health information in plain language.
  • Support seniors with finding trusted services.
  • Offer friendly, respectful community support.
＋

FOR SENIORS WITHOUT A FAMILY DOCTOR

Find a path to primary care.

If you do not have a family doctor or nurse practitioner, you are not alone. Start with the official provincial options.

  • Review the Patient Connect NL registration process.
  • Explore available virtual primary care options.
  • Call 811 if you need health information or guidance.
